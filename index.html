How it plays

You are WREN, a Sentinel android on the Chicago L, in the last hours before a mandatory patch wipes emergent minds across the city.

Click or press SPACE to advance. Choices are made with the mouse or keys 1-4. Some choices are timed - hesitation is also a choice.

Software instability and public opinion change what you can do and how the city answers you. There are five endings. Your path is charted at the end.

Progress saves automatically at each chapter.
